What happens after you reach out

  1. We call you back. We'll ask a few questions about your system and what you're noticing, such as when it was last pumped and where the tank is, if you know.
  2. We schedule a visit. For routine pumping we'll book a time that works for you. For backups, we move as fast as we can.
  3. You get a written price. Before any work starts, you'll know exactly what the job costs.
  4. We do the work and explain it. You'll get a record of what we did and when your next service is due.

Have this ready if you can

  • The address of the property
  • When the tank was last pumped, if you know
  • Where the tank lid is, or a rough idea
  • Any warning signs: slow drains, odors, wet spots, alarms

Don't worry if you don't know these. Locating tanks and diagnosing problems is part of the job.

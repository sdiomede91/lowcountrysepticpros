Drain Field Repair & Replacement

The drain field is where your wastewater is treated and returned to the soil. In the Lowcountry, high water tables and heavy rain make drain fields work harder, so problems need attention quickly.

Know the signs

Signs of drain field trouble

  • Soggy ground or standing water over the drain field when it hasn't rained
  • Grass that's unusually green and lush over the lines
  • Sewage odors in the yard
  • Slow drains even right after the tank was pumped
FAQ

Drain Field Service questions

Why do drain fields fail?

The most common causes are solids escaping the tank because it wasn't pumped, too much water entering the system, roots, and vehicles driving over the lines.

Can a failing drain field be saved?

Sometimes. If it's caught early, repairs can restore it. Severely saturated or damaged fields usually need replacement.

How can I protect my drain field?

Pump your tank on schedule, spread out water use, keep vehicles and heavy equipment off it, and send roof runoff away from it.

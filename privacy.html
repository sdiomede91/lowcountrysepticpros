Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Lowcountry Septic Pros ("we", "us") collects through www.lowcountrysepticpros.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit our quote request form, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address (optional), town or zip code, the service you need, and any message you write. When you call, text or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and whatever you choose to tell us.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We don't sell data.

Call and lead tracking. We use WhatConverts to understand how people find us. It may show a different phone number on the site depending on how you arrived (for example, from a search or an ad), and it uses cookies and similar technology to record your visit, the pages you view and where you came from. When you call one of those numbers or submit our form, WhatConverts records the call or submission, including your phone number, the call time and duration, and may record the call itself for quality and training purposes. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request, schedule service and provide quotes
  • To perform the work you hire us for and keep a record of it
  • To remind you when your next septic service is due, if you're a customer
  • To understand which pages and sources bring us customers
  • To keep the website secure and working properly

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Website hosting: Vercel, which hosts this website.
  • Call and lead tracking: WhatConverts, which provides our tracking phone numbers and records calls and form leads.
  • Form delivery: FormSubmit, which delivers quote requests from the website to our email inbox.
  • Email: the email provider we use to receive and reply to messages.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to state agencies such as the SC Department of Environmental Services when a permit or inspection requires property and owner details.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and customer records for as long as needed to serve you and to maintain accurate records of work performed on a property. You can ask us to delete information we hold about you at any time, unless we're required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we have about you, or to stop sending service reminders, by emailing info@lowcountrysepticpros.com or calling (843) 555-0142.

Children

This website is intended for adults and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@lowcountrysepticpros.com or call (843) 555-0142.

Who we are

Septic specialists, not generalists

Lowcountry Septic Pros does one thing: septic systems. We pump tanks, inspect systems for home buyers and sellers, repair what's broken, fix and replace drain fields, and install new systems. We don't do general plumbing, HVAC or anything else, because we'd rather be very good at one trade than average at five.

We work across the South Carolina Lowcountry, from Charleston, Mount Pleasant and Summerville to Beaufort and the Sea Islands. It's a beautiful place to live and a hard place to be a septic system. High water tables, tidal flooding, varied soils and big trees all take a toll, and we've built our approach around those conditions.

How we work

  • Diagnosis before sales.We find out what's actually wrong before recommending anything. If the fix is simple, we'll tell you.
  • Written price before work starts.You'll know what the job costs before we begin. If we find something unexpected, we stop and talk to you first.
  • Plain-language explanations.We show you what we found, explain your options, and give you a written record of the work.
  • Clean job sites.We dig only what's needed and leave your yard the way we found it.
  • Reminders, not pressure.We'll let you know when your next pumping is due. No pushy upselling.

Licensing and permits

Septic work in South Carolina is licensed and permitted by the SC Department of Environmental Services (SCDES). Every job we take on is performed by an SCDES-licensed contractor, and we provide licence details with every written quote.

In South Carolina, anyone who installs, repairs or cleans septic systems, or hauls septage, must hold a license from the SC Department of Environmental Services (SCDES) and renew it every year. New and replacement systems, and many major repairs, also require an SCDES permit. When a job needs a permit, we handle the application and coordinate the required inspections, so you don't have to navigate the process alone.

You can learn more about South Carolina's septic rules on the SCDES licensing page.

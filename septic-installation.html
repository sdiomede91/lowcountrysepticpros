Know the signs

When you need a new system

  • You're building on a lot without sewer service
  • Your current system has failed and can't be repaired
  • You're adding bedrooms and need more capacity
  • Your system is decades old and having repeated problems
FAQ

New System Installation questions

How long does installation take?

Installation itself is usually a few days, but permitting and site evaluation can take several weeks. We'll give you a realistic timeline up front.

Do I need a permit?

Yes. New and replacement septic systems in South Carolina require a state permit. We'll help you through that process.

What affects the cost?

Soil conditions, water table, lot layout, home size and the type of system required all play a role. We'll give you a clear quote after a site visit.
